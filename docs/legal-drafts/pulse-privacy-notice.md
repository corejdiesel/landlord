# Occupancy check-in — tenant privacy notice — DRAFT, NOT LEGALLY REVIEWED

**Status: draft produced by an automated build. Not reviewed. Do not publish.**

Written to be read by a tenant on a phone, in under a minute.

---

## What we asked you

Two things: how many people live in your home permanently, and how many
households that is.

## Why

Your landlord has to tell the government how many people live in a rented home,
and keep that up to date. Rather than guess, they asked you.

## What we store

Two numbers and the date you answered. That is all.

We do **not** store your name, your date of birth, your phone number, your email
address, or anything else about you. We do not know who answered — only that
someone with the link did.

## Who sees it

Your landlord, and their letting agent if they use one. Nobody else.

## Do you have to answer?

No. If you would rather not, tell your landlord and they can record it another
way.
