# Legal drafts — NOT REVIEWED

Everything in this folder is a **draft written by an automated build**. None of it
has been read by a solicitor or a data protection adviser. Do not publish any of
it, and do not rely on any of it.

They exist so that the shape of each document is decided and the gaps are
visible, not because they are ready.

| File | What it is | What it needs |
|---|---|---|
| `privacy-notice.md` | Landlord-facing privacy notice | Legal review, a real controller identity, a retention decision |
| `pulse-privacy-notice.md` | Tenant-facing notice for the occupancy check-in | Legal review; the lawful basis is the open question |
| `retention-schedule.md` | How long we keep what | A commercial decision on the six-year figure, then review |
| `dpia-outline.md` | Data protection impact assessment outline | Completion by a human; this is a skeleton, not an assessment |
