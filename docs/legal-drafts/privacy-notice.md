## What we collect, and why

**Information about you**
- Your name and email address, so you can sign in and we can send reminders.
- Your correspondence address, because the government register requires one and
  we prepare your answers for it.
- Your company or trust details, where you let through one.

**Information about your properties**
- Address, type, ownership, number of bedrooms and storeys, whether there is gas.
- Licensing details where a council requires a licence.
- Your registration numbers once you have registered.

**Information about your lettings**
- Rent, how often it is paid, whether bills are included.
- The number of households and occupants. We do not collect tenants' names.
- Deposit details and the scheme used.

**Documents you give us**
- Gas safety records, electrical reports, EPCs, licences and similar. We store the
  file and the details we read from it.

**A record of what you did**
- An append-only log of uploads, confirmations, reminders we sent, and changes you
  told us about. This is the record you can export, and it is the reason we keep it.

## What we do not collect

We do not collect tenants' names, dates of birth, contact details or financial
information. The occupancy check-in collects two numbers and nothing else — see the
separate tenant notice.
